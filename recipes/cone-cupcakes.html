These cone cupcakes are a fun and creative twist on traditional cupcakes. Perfect for parties and celebrations!

Ingredients
  • 1 box of cake mix (any flavor)
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 large eggs
  • Ice cream cones (flat-bottomed)
  • Frosting (store-bought or homemade)
  • Sprinkles or other decorations (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven according to the cake mix instructions.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cake mix, water, vegetable oil, and eggs. Mix until smooth and well combined.
  3. Place the ice cream cones on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat.
  4. Fill each cone about 2/3 full with the cake batter. Be careful not to overfill, as the batter will rise during baking.
  5. Bake the cone cupcakes in the preheated oven for the time specified on the cake mix box, usually around 15-20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Remove the cone cupcakes from the oven and let them cool completely on a wire rack.
  7. Once cooled, frost the cupcakes with your favorite frosting and decorate with sprinkles or other decorations as desired.
  8. Serve and enjoy your fun and delicious cone cupcakes!
