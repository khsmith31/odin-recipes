Bread

Delicious homemade bread made with simple ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp) active dry yeast
  • 1 1/4 cups warm water (110°F/45°C)
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  1. In a small bowl, dissolve sugar in warm water and sprinkle yeast on top. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes until frothy.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour and salt. Make a well in the center and pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for about 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Punch down the dough and shape it into a loaf. Place it on a greased baking sheet or in a loaf pan.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the bread is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom.
  7. Remove from the oven and let it cool on a wire rack before slicing.
